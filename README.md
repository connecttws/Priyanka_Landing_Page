# Priyanka — Thyroid-Focused Nutritionist Landing Page

A modern, high-converting Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS landing page built specifically for Priyanka, a Thyroid-Focused Nutritionist helping women manage thyroid symptoms, boost energy, and achieve healthy weight management through personalized nutrition.

---

## 🔗 Repository

- **GitHub Repository**: [https://github.com/connecttws/Priyanka_Landing_Page](https://github.com/connecttws/Priyanka_Landing_Page)

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Color Palette

- **Theme Style**: Light, luminous, clinical yet warm wellness palette (*"prefer lightish"*).
- **Background Canvas**: Soft ivory / porcelain pearl (`#fafaf7`).
- **Primary Accent**: Botanical therapeutic teal (`#0d9488` / `#0f766e`) with soft mint washes (`#f0fdfa`).
- **Headings & Contrast**: Deep Forest Evergreen (`#13382e`) for high typographic contrast and warmth.
- **Warm Accents**: Soft apricot/amber (`#e08338`) and blush rose (`#f43f5e`) for empathetic symptom highlights.
- **Typography**:
  - **Sans**: Plus Jakarta Sans (ultra-clean, humanist geometry).
  - **Serif Display**: Playfair Display (editorial elegance).
  - **Signature**: Great Vibes (authentic personal signature for Priyanka).

## 📋 Content & Section Breakdown

1. **Sticky Header (`Navbar.tsx`)**:
   - Signature logo, smooth-scroll links, and desktop/mobile "Book Free Consultation" trigger.
2. **Hero Section (`HeroSection.tsx`)**:
   - Primary Headline: *"Taking Thyroid Medication, But Not Feeling Like Yourself Again?"*
   - Subheadline explaining Priyanka's personalized nutrition approach.
   - VSL Video Box with custom play state and embedded masterclass player.
   - Primary CTA: *"Book Your Free Consultation"*.
   - Trust metrics: *1000+ women guided | 5+ years experience | Certified Thyroid-Focused Nutritionist*.
3. **Trust & Credentials Strip (`TrustStrip.tsx`)**:
   - Clean 5-point credibility markers.
4. **"Sound Familiar?" Empathy Section (`SymptomsSection.tsx`)**:
   - 4 Interactive symptom cards:
     - Tired all day, even after a full night's sleep
     - Weight keeps climbing, no matter how clean you eat
     - Hair loss, dull skin, and mood swings
     - Your daily life seems like an impossible task
   - Reassurance quote card: *"You are not lazy. Your body is asking for help, and a tablet alone was never meant to answer all of it."*
5. **"How It Works" Protocol (`HowItWorksSection.tsx`)**:
   - Subtitle: *"Your struggles, your story"*
   - Phase 1: **IDENTIFY** — What might be affecting you (reports, gut, stress).
   - Phase 2: **PERSONALISE** — A personalised food and lifestyle plan that fits your life.
   - Phase 3: **SUSTAIN** — Regular follow-ups so you are never alone.
   - Secondary CTA: *"Book Your Free Thyroid Consultation"*.
6. **Coach Profile (`CoachProfileSection.tsx`)**:
   - Headline: *"Meet Priyanka, THYROID-FOCUSED NUTRITIONIST"*
   - Professional photo showcase container.
   - Bio: 5+ years experience, no crash diets, customized Indian kitchen meals.
   - Mission banner: *"My mission is to help every woman with thyroid feel energetic, confident, and in control of her health again."*
7. **Social Proof & Client Stories (`TestimonialSection.tsx`)**:
   - Headline: *"Real Women, Real Change"*
   - 3 detailed case studies with metric badges (Energy, Weight, Symptoms).
   - Authentic WhatsApp screenshot carousel with full-screen lightbox preview.
8. **Frequently Asked Questions (`FAQSection.tsx`)**:
   - Smooth accordion with answers to:
     - Do I have to stop my medicine? (No, decided only with your doctor).
     - How soon will I see results? (Most women notice better energy in 3-6 weeks).
     - Is the consultation really free? (Yes, 100% free with no obligation).
     - Nutrition & lifestyle compatibility questions.
9. **Final Conversion Section (`FinalCTASection.tsx`)**:
   - Headline: *"How many more years will you live tired and frustrated?"*
   - Subheadline: *"Take the first step today and find out what your thyroid is really telling you."*
   - Button: *"Book My Free Consultation"*.
   - Reassurance: *"Your details are 100% safe. Limited slots every week."*
10. **Interactive Booking Modal (`BookingModal.tsx`)**:
    - Collects Full Name, WhatsApp Number, Email, Thyroid Condition, Primary Struggle, and Preferred Call Window.
    - Confirmation screen with WhatsApp follow-up details.
11. **Floating Mobile Bar**:
    - Sticky pill allowing one-tap booking anywhere on the page.
